# TripNest AI: User Guide

Version 2.0 · 2 November 2026. No account is needed.

## What TripNest AI tells you
How crowded places near you are **predicted** to be on the date you choose, the calmest time of day to visit, and lesser-known places worth visiting instead. The numbers are an ML prediction trained on demonstration data, not live counts.

## Start
1. Open the home page. Nothing asks for your location yet.
2. **Location.** Press **Use my current location** (your browser asks permission first) or type a city and pick it from the list. If you refuse permission, simply type a city.
3. **Date** (default: the next Saturday) and an optional **interest**, such as Beaches or Heritage.
4. Press **Explore Destinations**. The heading says, for example, "Discover amazing places around Coimbatore."

## The results page
- **Popular places near you.** Photo, state, distance, a crowd chip (green quiet, yellow moderate, red high), best time, short description, **View Details**.
- **Best time to visit.** Pick a place to see four parts of the day (early morning, morning, afternoon, evening), the calmest one highlighted, and a line such as "Best time to visit: 8:00 AM – 9:00 AM". If the data cannot support a recommendation, none is given.
- **Escape the Crowds.** Lesser-known places that are predicted to be calm on your date, each with the reason it was chosen.
- **Crowd map.** Your position, popular places and hidden gems with different markers. Filters: All, Popular, Hidden gems, Low, Moderate, High. Tap a marker for a photo, level, best time and **View Details**. "View on map" on a card jumps here.
- The bar at the top changes the location, date, interest or time of day and refreshes everything.

Crowd levels are shares of an *assumed* capacity. Distance is a straight line, not a road distance.

## The destination page
Choose a date and time range for the predicted level, the number of people with a likely range, the four-part timeline, the best time, an hourly chart, seven day tiles, the places inside, a map, and **About this prediction** (when it was made, what it is built from, limitations). "Why we showed you this" appears only when you arrived from a search.

## If something goes wrong
| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Location field asks for a city | Type a city, or allow location |
| "We couldn't find …" | Choose a suggestion or a nearby larger city |
| Date message | Pick a date within the next 18 months |
| "No best time recommended" | The place is busy all day or open too briefly; try another date |
| A neutral box instead of a photo | The photo host is unreachable; everything else works |
| Map missing | The map service needs internet access |

## Privacy
No sign-in. Nothing about you is stored. A shared location is rounded to about 1 km, used only to find places near you, and not kept.
